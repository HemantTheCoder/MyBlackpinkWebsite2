Hey! I'm Hemant, a Civil Engineering student and a proud BLINK.

What started as casually listening to a few BLACKPINK songs quickly turned into following their music, performances, solo projects, and everything in between. Their talent, hard work, stage presence, and ability to connect with millions of fans worldwide are what made me a fan.

I created this website as a small tribute to BLACKPINK and the amazing community that surrounds them. Whether it's iconic performances, memorable moments, music releases, or simply appreciating the members and their journeys, this site is my way of sharing that appreciation.

When I'm not working on engineering projects, research papers, or GIS maps, you'll probably find me listening to BLACKPINK, exploring new music, or updating this website.

Thanks for stopping by, and as BLACKPINK says,
"BLACKPINK in your area!" 🖤💗
